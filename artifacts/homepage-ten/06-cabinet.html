A note from the cabinet

Useful is a very personal thing.
Your apps can be, too.

For the things that matter
to you. And perhaps
a few people you know.

i.

Tell your assistant.

Connect Claude or ChatGPT, then describe what you need in your own words. Your assistant builds your app.

ii.

Give it a home.

yaks.app hosts it at your own address. Your data, photos and files stay with your app, ready on any device.

iii.

Let it grow.

Share a link with someone. Invite them to edit. Ask your assistant for changes whenever you have another idea.
