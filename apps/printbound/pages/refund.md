> **Plain-language note:** Every book is printed for one person, so there is
> nothing to restock. This page says in plain English when you get your money
> back and when you don't. We link it before you pay.

Every PrintBound book is custom-made to order from your content. Because each
book is printed just for you, it can't be restocked or resold — so our policy is
built around making it right, not taking returns.

## Custom print-on-demand goods

Your book is a **custom, made-to-order item**. Once it has entered production at
our print partner, it **cannot be returned, exchanged, or refunded for buyer's
remorse, a change of mind, or a typo or error in the content you submitted**.
Please review your content carefully before you order.

## When we give a full refund

We will issue a **full refund (or, at your choice, a free reprint)** if your
order is:

- **Defective** — a printing or binding flaw, such as misbound pages, missing or
  blank pages, or a damaged cover;
- **Wrong** — the book you received is not the book you ordered;
- **Lost in transit** — the carrier confirms it was lost or it never arrives;
- **Cancelled by us** — we refuse or cancel your order for any reason (see our
  [Terms of Service](terms)); or
- **Late** — we fail to ship within the time we represented and you do not wish
  to wait.

For defective, wrong, or damaged books, contact us within **30 days** of
delivery at [hello@printbound.net](mailto:hello@printbound.net) with your order
reference and a photo of the problem. You won't need to ship the book back
unless we ask.

## Cancelling an order

Because production starts quickly, you can cancel for a full refund **only
during the brief window after payment and before we send your print-ready file
to our print partner** for production. Once your file has been submitted to
production, the order can no longer be cancelled, though the full-refund
situations above still apply. To request a cancellation, email
[hello@printbound.net](mailto:hello@printbound.net) as soon as possible with
your order reference; we'll cancel and refund it if production has not yet
started.

## How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method through Stripe. It
may take a few business days for your bank or card issuer to post the refund.
This policy is disclosed to you, and linked, **before you pay**, and it does not
limit any rights you may have under applicable consumer-protection law.

## Shipping window

Your quote shows the selected shipping option and estimated delivery window for
your destination. Your receipt shows progress confirmed by the print provider.
If we cannot fulfill your paid order, contact us to arrange a replacement or
refund.

## Contact

Questions about a refund or return? Email
[hello@printbound.net](mailto:hello@printbound.net).
