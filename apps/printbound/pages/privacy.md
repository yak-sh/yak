PrintBound is operated by Yak Shaving LLC. We use the text, images and documents
you submit to prepare your book, and the name, email, phone and destination you
provide to fulfill your order. Your drafts and documents stay private.

## Payment and printing

PrintBound is the print seller. Stripe processes your payment; we do not store
full payment-card numbers. Lulu receives the document and delivery details
needed to print and ship the order. yaks.app provides hosting, private storage
and account access.

## Your choices

We do not sell your data. A draft is kept so you can return to it. Contact
[hello@printbound.net](mailto:hello@printbound.net) to request access,
corrections or deletion; payment records may need to be retained for accounting
and legal obligations.

## Messages

Creating a draft or making a purchase does not subscribe you to marketing email.
Existing unsubscribe choices remain in force. Order and support messages are
separate from marketing.

## Children

Adults submit books and make purchases. Do not submit someone else's personal
details or images without permission. A parent or guardian must give permission
for a child's image.
