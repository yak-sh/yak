Frequently asked questions

How does it work?

Save your idea, choose a style and edit the story and page descriptions. Review your generated pages, print a free page at home, or prepare the book for a PrintBound quote.

Can I return to a book?

Your personal shelf keeps saved drafts and completed artwork. Return to a draft to continue editing or resume unfinished pages.

Can I print a page at home?

Yes. Your free printable is separate from buying a bound book.

What does a book cost?

PrintBound quotes the selected document, quantity, destination and shipping. Review the total before checkout. CrayonBloom does not add a second print charge.

Who makes the books?

Adults create the books and order them. Kids color the pages. A first name is optional for personalization.

What if something goes wrong?

A failed drawing stays unfinished and can be retried without losing completed pages. Contact us for help with artwork; a print receipt identifies the seller for cancellations, shipping and refunds.

Is my artwork public?

No. Gallery sharing needs separate consent for the selected pages. It is optional.
