True things about unicorns

Medieval Europe bought and sold unicorn horns.

They were narwhal tusks, traded widely, whole and cut into pieces. Cleveland Museum of Art

A narwhal's horn is really a tooth.

In males, the left tooth of the upper jaw grows forward and out through the front of the jaw — up to about ten feet of it. NOAA Fisheries

Scotland's national animal is the unicorn.

It first turned up on the Scottish royal coat of arms around the 12th century. Scotland.org
