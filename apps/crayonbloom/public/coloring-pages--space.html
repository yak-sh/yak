Space facts, in case they ask

Saturn is not the only planet with rings.

Jupiter has them too — hard to see unless the sun is behind them. A good excuse to color rings on more than one. NASA Science

The astronauts' tracks are still on the moon.

No wind, no rain to wear them away. A NASA orbiter photographed the ones Apollo 15 left, thirty-nine years later. NASA Science

The sun is a star, just the closest one.

The next nearest is Proxima Centauri, and its light takes 4.24 years to get here. NASA Science

A day on Jupiter is under 10 hours.

The biggest planet is also the fastest spinner — around once every 9.9 hours. NASA Science
