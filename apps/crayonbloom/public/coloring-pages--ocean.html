Ocean facts worth saying out loud

An octopus has three hearts.

Two pump blood to the gills to pick up oxygen; the third sends it round the rest of the body. NOAA Ocean Service

Octopus blood is blue.

The protein that carries its oxygen is built on copper, not the iron that makes ours red. Natural History Museum

A blue whale is the biggest animal that has ever lived.

Bigger than any dinosaur. NOAA Fisheries

A blue whale's heart weighs about 400 pounds.

About as much as two grown-ups. Only one has ever been preserved. Royal Ontario Museum, via Earth Touch News

Sea turtles go back to the coast where they hatched.

Every stretch of coast has its own magnetic signature, and a turtle steers by it — sometimes across a whole ocean. Lohmann Lab, UNC

A summer flounder takes the color of the sea floor it lies on.

Which earns it the nickname chameleon of the sea, and is a fine excuse to color the same fish two different ways. NOAA Fisheries
