Refund & Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Digital downloads

One custom sample page is free to download as a printable PDF. A full-book PDF is included with a paid printed-book order; it is not sold separately, so there is no separate download charge to refund.

What you pay

The price shown at checkout is the final total — shipping and any applicable tax are calculated and shown before you confirm, so there are no surprise charges afterward.

Physical prints — shipping

  • Printing and shipping are currently available in the United States only.
  • Each book is printed on demand by PrintBound, our print service after you order, then shipped to the address you provide. Please double-check your shipping address at checkout — we can't reroute a package once it has shipped.
  • Review your completed document before you buy. Shipping options and the estimated delivery window are shown with your PrintBound quote. Your receipt shows progress confirmed by the print provider.

If we can't make your book, you get your money back

If a book fails on our end before it ships — the drawing stops partway, or the finished file doesn't pass our print checks — you get a full refund. The same goes if your order sits past the timeline above and nothing has shipped. There is nothing to send back and nothing to prove.

  • Email hello@crayonbloom.com from the address you ordered with, or tell us the book's title, and say you'd like a refund. We answer refund requests within 2 business days.
  • We refund the full amount you paid — book, shipping, and tax — back to the payment method you used. Banks usually take another 5 to 10 business days to post it.
  • If you'd rather we try again, say so and we'll redraw the book at no extra charge. Asking costs you nothing: if the second try fails, or you change your mind while you wait, the refund is still yours.

A book that fails on our end alerts a person here, but you never have to wait on us to notice. Write to us and we'll refund it.

Physical prints — refunds & replacements

Once a book is made it is custom to your kid, so we don't accept returns for a change of heart or for typos and mistakes in the idea you submitted. If the book itself goes wrong, we fix it:

  • If your book arrives damaged, defective, or printed incorrectly on our end, contact us within 30 days of delivery with a photo and we'll reprint or refund it at no cost to you.
  • If your order never arrives, contact us and we'll work with the carrier to resolve it (replacement or refund).

For a PrintBound order, your receipt identifies the seller. To start a claim, email hello@crayonbloom.com with your order details. We answer claims within 2 business days.
