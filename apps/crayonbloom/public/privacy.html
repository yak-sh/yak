Privacy Policy

CrayonBloom is operated by Yak Shaving LLC. Adults create accounts and books; children do the coloring.

Information we use

We keep your saved ideas, prompts, selected style, references, story edits and artwork so you can return to a draft. Account access and private storage are provided by yaks.app. An optional first name can personalize a book; do not include a child's last name, birthdate or photo.

Generation and printing

Your prompts and selected reference images are sent to the generation provider to make your pages. PrintBound prepares the selected print document and sells the printed book. Its checkout identifies the seller and shows the destination, shipping and total. Stripe processes payment, and Lulu prints and ships the book. We do not store full card numbers.

Sharing and email

Your books and artwork are private unless you give separate permission to publish selected pages in the gallery. A gallery choice never grants permission to publish other pages. Marketing email is optional and separate from saving a page or ordering. Existing opt-outs remain in force; every marketing message must offer an unsubscribe choice. Service messages are separate.

Your choices

We do not sell personal information. For access, correction, deletion, gallery removal or privacy questions, email hello@crayonbloom.com. Records required for accounting or legal obligations may need to be retained.
