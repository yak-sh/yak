Dinosaur facts to drop while they color

Nobody is sure what the plates on a stegosaurus were for.

Warning off predators, telling each other apart, and controlling body heat are all still guesses. A good excuse for bright colors. Natural History Museum

Triceratops grew new teeth its whole life.

The old ones wore down slicing through tough plants, and fresh ones kept pushing up behind them. Erickson et al., Science Advances

The biggest dinosaurs were plant-eaters, not the toothy hunters.

Patagotitan outweighed Tyrannosaurus rex at least six times over, on a diet of leaves. Natural History Museum

Brachiosaurus could not chew.

That long neck reached the leaves at the very top of the tree, and down they went whole. Natural History Museum

Pteranodon was a flying reptile, not a dinosaur.

A cousin of the dinosaurs, and a fact your kid will treasure correcting you on later. Natural History Museum
