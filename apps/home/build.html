How yaks.app works

  1. Describe it

    Type what you need in the box above.

  2. Your Operator builds it

    Your Operator is an AI helper that does the work. It builds the app and puts it online while you watch. Open it on your phone or computer, or send its link to the people it's for.

  3. Sign up free to keep it

    Enter your email, and the app is yours, at your own address like yourname.yaks.app.

  4. Change it by asking

    Want a new column, a new page or a fix? Tell your Operator in plain words, by chat on yaks.app or by email. Give it your mobile number, and you can text it or call it.

Already use Claude or ChatGPT? Paste yaks.app/agent into it, and it can build your apps too.
