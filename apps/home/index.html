Change anything by asking.

Ask for a new field, a photo on every item or another page. Your assistant makes the change, checks the result and puts the new version online.

  • Ask in chat, by email, or by text from your phone
  • Photos and files, up to 50 GB on the Plus plan
  • Earlier versions are kept, and going back never touches your data

Connect the services you already use.

Apps can use Google Calendar, Google Sheets, Slack, Notion, QuickBooks and more. You sign in to the service once. yaks.app keeps the connection for the app.

  • Sign in on the service’s own page, with nothing to copy or paste
  • Each visitor can connect an account of their own
  • Remove a connection at any time, and your app stops using the service

Send email to your app’s users.

Thank-yous, reminders, receipts and news go out from your app’s own address. Visitors can sign up for notices with only an email address, and every notice carries a one-click unsubscribe link.

  • Reminders on a schedule, like every Monday at 7 am
  • Notices to everyone who asked, like “Back in stock”
  • See pricing for the email allowance on each plan

Every app has an email address.

Forward the bulletin, a booking or the week’s specials. An app can file the message with its attachments and show the news on its page.

Take payments with Stripe.

On the Plus plan, your app can sell through Stripe, the payment company: orders, tickets, deposits and gifts, paid by card on Stripe’s own checkout page. The money goes to your Stripe account, and each buyer gets a receipt by email from your app.

  • Every order listed in your app, for you and your editors
  • Refunds and payouts handled in your own Stripe account
  • Buyers pay on Stripe’s checkout page

An AI helper that answers your visitors.

Give your app a helper that answers the questions visitors ask: the allergens on a menu, parking at the church, what’s on for Tuesday of the trip. The helper answers from what your app keeps.

  • AI use is included on every plan
  • You choose how much of your monthly AI budget visitors may spend
  • Each visitor has a daily limit, so no stranger spends the whole budget

Invite your app’s users, free.

Members, editors and visitors cost nothing on any plan. Members of a private app sign in with a code sent to their email, so nobody makes or forgets a password, and everything a sign-up sheet, a shop or a directory needs is already built.

  • Invite members to one app or the whole space
  • Choose who can view and who can edit
  • Members use a sign-in code sent by email

Common questions, answered.

Do I need to know how to code?

You describe what you need in your own words. The assistant writes the code and puts your app online. If you write code, you can read and change every file.

Who can see what is in my app?

Choose who can open an app and who can change it. People you invite to a private app sign in with a code sent to their email.

What happens when I reach a limit?

Your apps and data stay. You may have to wait for room to add something, such as a new app or another email. Visitors can still open the apps you share.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your paid plan runs to the end of the period you paid for. Then your space returns to the free plan’s limits, and your apps and data stay.

Can I take my data with me?

Ask for the data in an app as a spreadsheet, or delete the app whenever you like.
