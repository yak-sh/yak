Build it, host it and share it here

Build an app by saying what you need
Describe it in plain words, and yaks.app builds it. For a new feature or a fix, ask your Operator, the AI assistant that comes with your apps, and it makes the change.
It stays online and saves what people add
Your app lives at your own address, like yourname.yaks.app, on any phone or computer. Sign-ups, recipes, photos and files are saved for everyone you share with.
Share it with the people it's for
Keep an app to yourself, send its link, or invite as many people as you like to view or edit it, free. Publish it, and others can get a copy of their own.

How yaks.app works

  1. Say what you need

    Type it in the box above, like a chore chart for your household.

  2. Open it anywhere

    yaks.app builds it and puts it online. Open it on your phone or computer, or send its link to the people it's for.

  3. Sign up free to keep it

    Enter your email, and the app is yours, at your own address like yourname.yaks.app.

Already use Claude or ChatGPT? Paste yaks.app/agent into it, and it can build your apps too.
