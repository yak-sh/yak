The office asks, and the change is made

  1. It starts from the site you have

    Type your website's address above. The preview has what your site says: who you are, where and when, and what's coming up. It has the words but not the pictures, and you send those to the assistant afterward. A preview you don't keep is deleted after 7 days.

  2. You ask for changes by email or chat

    Write the way you would to a person on staff: “Here are Tuesday's minutes. Please post them.” The assistant makes the change and writes back to say what it did.

  3. It goes online at your own web address

    Until then only you can see the preview. When you are ready you choose a plan, and the assistant walks you through pointing your web address, like yourorganization.org, at the new site. It is the one technical step. Then the new site takes the old one's place there.

We watch for mistakes and fix them

The assistant is AI, and AI gets things wrong. This is what happens when it does.

A broken page or form is recorded as it happens
When a page fails on a visitor's phone, or a form doesn't save, it is written down that moment. The assistant starts on it right away: it finds the cause, fixes it and puts the fix online. When you sign in, you see each one and what was done about it.
When the fault is ours, we fix it
Sometimes the cause is our own software and not your site. We are told as it happens, and we fix it. The assistant can also report a problem to us on your behalf, and our answer comes back to you.
A wrong fact is put right by asking
No software can know that a meeting date is wrong. Each time it changes something, the assistant tells you what it changed, so you can check it. Reply that it is wrong, and it is corrected.
Any change can be put back
The last 20 versions of your site are kept, so you can ask for it as it was before a change. Sign-ups or directory entries changed or lost by mistake can be restored as they stood at any time in the last 30 days, and anything deleted stays in the trash for 30 days.
The site and what is in it are yours
Ask for everything in it as a file or a spreadsheet whenever you like, or delete it. We never sell what is in it or share it with advertisers, as the privacy page says. There is no contract. If you stop paying, the plan runs to the end of the month you paid for. After that nothing is deleted, and the site has the Free plan's limits, which don't include your own web address.
