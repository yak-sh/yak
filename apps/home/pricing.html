How paying works

A space is one address and everything in it.
That's its apps, their data and its people. Each space has its own plan and its own limits, and what one space uses never counts against another.
Your first space is free, and you can join as many as you like.
You can own one free space. Every other space you make is on the Plus plan or the Pro plan. Spaces other people invite you into never count toward yours. If you already own more than one free space, they all stay free.
A space's owner pays for it, and members never do.
Only an owner can upgrade a space, change its plan or cancel it, from that space's settings. Editors and viewers never pay, and there's no charge for each person.
At a limit, nothing is deleted.
Adding stops: a new app, more data or another email waits until the space has room or moves to a paid plan. The owner is told which limit the space reached and how to upgrade, and your assistant hears when a space nears one. Members keep using everything, and visitors are always served.
If you stop paying, the space goes back to free.
A paid plan runs to the end of the month you paid for. Then the space has the free plan's limits again and stops taking payments. Its apps and data stay.
Your Claude or ChatGPT plan pays for your assistant, and yaks.app keeps what it makes online.
Your assistant builds and changes your apps on your own subscription, and that never spends a space's AI use. A space's AI use is for the AI its apps use themselves, such as reading a photo or speaking, and for yaks.app's built-in builder.
Prices include tax.
Any sales tax or VAT is in the price. Nothing is added at checkout.
We take 0% of each sale.
Stripe charges its own processing fees. How selling works is below.

Mail to your apps always arrives.
At the email limit, only sending stops. The count starts again on the first day of each month.
Invitations are free, up to 50 an hour.
Invite as many people as you like, on any plan. Invitations never count toward the month's emails, and one person may send 50 an hour.
Visitors who aren't signed in have a per-minute pace.
Anyone not signed in can make up to 300 requests a minute for an app's data from one place, and past that is asked to wait a minute. You and the people in your space aren't counted.
Visitors to an open app keep a visitor's pace.
On an app anyone with the link can add to, each visitor may send 30 changes a minute, 16 KB at a time, and upload files of up to 2 MB. A visitor changes only what they added. You and your editors aren't held to these limits.
