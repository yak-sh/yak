It starts from your site, and you change it by asking

  1. You watch your new site being built

    A chat opens and says what it is doing. Your new site shows as it is made, the page itself after a minute or two, and it is ready to open in about 3 minutes. The site you have now is only read. Nothing on it changes.

  2. Keep it, and put it online

    If you like what you see, give your email and it is yours, free. If you don't, it is deleted after 7 days. To put it online, connect your church's web address to it: a record you add where that address is registered shows the site is yours, and only then can anyone else open it.

  3. Ask for changes by email or chat

    Your church gets an Operator, an AI helper that does the building. Ask it the way you would ask a volunteer who is good with computers: “Put the Christmas Eve service times on the home page.” It makes the change and writes back.

  4. Only the people you invite see what is private

    Members sign in with their email to open the directory, and nobody else can. What is in it stays yours, to take with you or delete whenever you like.
