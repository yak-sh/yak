From your first step to your first app

  1. We text you a code

    Type its six digits on the next page. There is no password to make and nothing to install.

  2. You meet your Operator

    You land in your own space, an address like yourname.yaks.app where your apps live, in a chat with your Operator.

  3. Ask for the app you want

    Like a chore chart for your household. Your Operator builds it and puts it online. Open it on any phone or computer, or send its link to the people it's for.

What your Operator does for you

It changes and fixes your apps
Ask for a new feature or a fix any time, and your Operator makes the change.
You can text it or call it
Agree to texts in its settings, then text or call your Operator from your phone. It texts you when it finishes a change or needs an answer from you.
Share anything it does or builds
Keep an app to yourself, share it with a link, or invite people to view or edit it, free.
