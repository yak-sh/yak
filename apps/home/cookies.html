Cookies

Last updated Nov 10th, 2026

yaks.app sets one cookie, a second one when an ad or a campaign link brought you, and a third when you open our home page. This page explains what they do.

The session cookie

We set yak_session when you use an app, on yaks.app and its subdomains. Before you sign in, it holds a signed identifier for the browser that writes, so you can keep editing what you wrote without signing in (guest entries). Signing in adds your identity and puts your name on those entries across the apps you wrote to. It expires after ninety days of inactivity, and using it renews it. We don't use it for visitor counts or advertising.

If you clear it, you're signed out, and the browser loses access to any guest entries you haven't claimed by signing in. If you refuse it, you can't stay signed in or keep your guest identity between requests.

The campaign cookie

We set yak_came on yaks.app only when the link you followed carries campaign tags (utm_source and the like) or an ad's click id (such as Meta's fbclid). It holds those tags, that id and when you arrived, and nothing else: no name, address or session. It lets us count which campaigns bring people who sign up, and tell the ad network whose ad you clicked that its click led to a visit or a sign-up (the privacy policy says exactly what). It expires after ninety days, and a later campaign link replaces it. Clearing or refusing it changes nothing you can see.

The home page cookie

We sometimes show different visitors different versions of our home page, to learn which one explains yaks.app best. When you open one, we set yak_home on yaks.app. It holds the name of the version you saw, and nothing else: no name, address or session. It lets us show you the same version next time, and count how many visitors to each version went on to sign up. It expires after ninety days. Clearing or refusing it changes only which version of the home page you see.

Other cookies

We don't set third-party cookies, and no ad network's code runs on our pages. Cloudflare, which hosts yaks.app, may set its own cookie to tell people from bots. That cookie is a security measure and isn't used to build a profile of you. Our privacy policy has more about what we collect.

Apps on yaks.app

Apps in a yaks.app space use the session cookie, and we set no other cookie for them.
