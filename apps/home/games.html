Change anything by asking.

Ask for a new level, a boss, a rule or a sound. Your assistant makes the change, checks the result and puts the new version online.

  • Art, music and other files, up to 50 GB on the Plus plan
  • Earlier versions are kept, and going back never touches your data
  • In chat

    Ask here, in Claude or in ChatGPT.

  • In Discord

    Mention your assistant in your server, and it answers there.

  • In Slack

    Mention your assistant in your Slack workspace, and it answers there.

  • By email

    Send your assistant the change and any files.

  • By text

    Ask for a change from your phone.

Your friends play from a link.

Send the link, and your friends play in their web browser, on a phone or a computer, with nothing to install. Everyone sees the same game at the same moment, and the game keeps your progress for next time.

  • Friends you invite sign in with a code sent to their email
  • Play with a keyboard, a touchscreen or a game controller
  • Friends play free on every plan

Characters who talk with your players.

Give your game characters who answer what players say to them: a shopkeeper, a guide, a rival. An AI writes each answer from what your game knows.

  • AI use is included on every plan
  • You choose how much of your monthly AI budget players may spend
  • Each player has a daily limit, so no stranger spends the whole budget

Your game is code you can open and change.

Your game is written in TypeScript and runs in the browser, with three.js for 3D. Read and change every file yourself, or ask your assistant. yaks.app saves the game and keeps every player in step, so there’s no server for you to run.

  • Add packages from npm
  • Ready-made parts for game controls, on-screen displays and 3D worlds
  • Build from Claude or ChatGPT as well as here

Common questions, answered.

Do I need to know how to code?

You describe what you need in your own words. The assistant writes the code and puts your app online. If you write code, you can read and change every file.

Do my friends have to pay?

No. Every plan has unlimited visitors, and friends you invite sign in with a code sent to their email.

What do my friends need to play?

A web browser on a phone or a computer. There’s nothing to download, and a game can take a keyboard, a touchscreen or a game controller.

What happens when I reach a limit?

Your apps and data stay. You may have to wait for room to add something, such as a new app or another email. Visitors can still open the apps you share.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your paid plan runs to the end of the period you paid for. Then your space returns to the free plan’s limits, and your apps and data stay.
