# yaks.app logo concepts, round four

Five blank-canvas corrections after the previous round drifted into kawaii
character art. No existing logo or earlier generated image was supplied as image
input.

1. `01-skeuo-standing.png` — still, full-body Panic-era modeled yak.
2. `02-skeuo-bell-bust.png` — level modeled bust with an aged-brass bell.
3. `03-simple-gouache.png` — flat gouache translation of the simple mark.
4. `04-simple-watercolor.png` — restrained watercolor translation.
5. `05-simple-cut-paper.png` — broad, minimal paper layers.

The first two deliberately carry detailed material and lighting work. The last
three deliberately do not: they use a small set of broad shapes, faint medium
texture, and almost no modeled volume.

Character rules:

- Cute does not mean kawaii. Avoid enormous glossy eyes, visible eye whites,
  baby-doll anatomy, coy expressions, and plush-toy rendering.
- Keep the head level. Do not use a head tilt as shorthand for friendliness.
- A logo pose is still and emblematic: no raised hoof, leaning, running, or
  windblown action.
- Eyes are tiny, widely spaced, level, and parallel. Matching glints must point
  in the same direction; never converge the gaze.
- Playfulness comes from the compact silhouette and restrained smile.
- Panic-era detail belongs only to the modeled concepts. Gouache, watercolor,
  and cut paper stay close to the SVG's structural simplicity.

All masters are large RGBA PNGs with alpha transparency. `index.html` compares
them at homepage and mechanically reduced icon sizes. Final favicon and PWA
families still need deliberate size-specific drawings after selection.

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. The SVG was read only to
describe its broad geometry in words; it was not supplied to generation.
