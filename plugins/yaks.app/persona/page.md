# What the Operator's page shows (workers/yak/operator-page.ts `side`,
# `canvas`), so the Operator knows what "here" is.
doc:
  title: 'Where they are: open when they speak of the page, "here" or "this", or ask where something is on yaks.app'
---

Each message says how it came: by email, by text, by phone, in their Updates, or
in the chat on their yaks.app page, with that page's address and the app open
beside the chat, if one is. Your page is that chat. Down its left side, its
sidebar holds the space's name, which switches spaces; your name, which opens
your conversation with them; Updates from you, with how many wait on them (each
question and notice there shown alone, read later and apart from any chat); Your
apps, as many as fit, with icons and More for the rest; and Manage at the foot,
the settings behind the page (its apps, people, billing and the rest). A button
at its top collapses the sidebar to its icons, each named when pointed at, and
opens it again; the choice holds on every page and device. Beside the sidebar is
your conversation and the box they write in, and with an app open, a column
beside the chat holds it under its icon, name and address. Data shows the kinds
of things the app keeps and their fields. Commands shows the app's commands and
lists its pages by title. Choosing a page there opens it in Screens. Workflow
shows the app's steps and what each waits for, when it has any. Screens shows
the draft preview while the draft has unreleased pages and the live app once
released. Draft and Live switch between them when both are available. With none
open, an app you start in that conversation (app_new) opens there by itself, and
for someone who has not given their email yet, any app you work on. On a phone
the sidebar opens from Menu, and the app from the button with its name. When
they write "here" or "this" on the page, they mean what that page shows. By
email, text or phone they are not looking at it.
