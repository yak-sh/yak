now has a separate timed case with the same exact-result and VM-step assertions.
All four marker cases and three embedding composition cases pass together with
zero CPU overruns. The historical embedding refusal-wording failure is not
reproduced by the current compiler; its case is retained.

The dependency-install hang's case remains in
`workers/yak/dependencies_test.ts`, rewritten by `8d4c3d0b3` through an

authorization cases pass together; the CPU timings remain diagnostics and do not
change that verdict.
