How yaks.app works

  1. Connect the assistant you use

    Paste yaks.app/agent into Claude, ChatGPT or the AI assistant you already use.

  2. Ask for the app you want

    Tell your assistant what you need, like a chore chart for your household. Your assistant builds the app, and yaks.app puts it online.

  3. Open your app on any device

    Your apps live in your own space at yourname.yaks.app. Open one on your phone or computer, or send its link to the people it's for.

Everything you make lives in a space

A space is an address like yourname.yaks.app, the apps there, their data and the people you invite. Each space is free or on the Plus plan.

It saves what people add
Recipes, sign-ups, comments, photos and files are saved online, on every phone and computer, for everyone you share with.
You choose who comes in
Keep an app to yourself, share it with a link, or invite as many people as you like to view or edit it, free.
Your assistant keeps working on it
Ask for a new feature or a fix right from the chat. When an app breaks, your assistant is told about the error.

From a tool for you to a business

Share it
Invite your team, your family or your customers. The people you invite are free on every plan.
Publish it
Put your app in the gallery, and anyone can get a copy of their own, with its own data.
Sell it
On the Plus plan, your apps take payments with Stripe and live at your own domain.
