How it works

  1. Connect your assistant

    Set up yaks.app in Claude or ChatGPT once. It takes a few minutes. Follow the setup steps.

  2. Describe what you need

    Ask for something in your own words. Your assistant builds it, and you can ask for changes as you go.

  3. Open it and use it

    Your app gets a link like yourname.yaks.app/recipes. Open it in your browser or send it to a friend. There's nothing to install.
