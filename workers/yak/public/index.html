And then it's yours

It opens on your phone.
Open the website, or tap the link somebody sent you. Nothing to install, no app store, no waiting.
It keeps what you put in it.
Everything you write stays, and it's the same on your laptop as on your phone. Change it on one and the other catches up.
Share it, or don't.
Send the link to one person, leave it open to whoever has it, or keep the whole thing to yourself. You choose, and you can change your mind.
Friends can write in it too.
Invite someone by email and they can add their own bit — a note on a recipe, a name next to Saturday, a photo of the tomatoes.
Upload photos or files.
Add pictures or files straight from your phone and they live in the app alongside everything else.
Your assistant can use it, not just build it.
An app can teach your assistant its own little jobs, so you can say add eight miles to the running board without opening the page at all.

How to connect
