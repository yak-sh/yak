How it works

  1. Sign in and connect your assistant

    Sign in with your email to get your own space at an address like yourname.yaks.app. Your apps, their data and the people you invite all live there. Then connect Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code or Cursor. It takes a few minutes, once.

  2. Ask for the app you want

    Tell your assistant, in your own words, what the app is for and who will use it: a practice planner for your team, a menu for your side business, a recipe box for your family. It builds the app and puts it online.

  3. Open it on any device

    Your app has its own link, like yourname.yaks.app/recipes. Open it in a browser on your phone or computer, or send the link to the people it's for. There's nothing to install.
