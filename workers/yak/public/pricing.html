What it costs

The free plan includes up to five apps. Plus costs $4 a month and includes unlimited apps and higher usage limits.

Plans

Free

Includes up to five apps.

  • 5 apps
  • 1 app built for you
  • 50,000 visits a month
  • 1 GB of app data
  • 100 emails a month, sent or received
  • Your own address at yourname.yaks.app

$4 a month

Unlimited apps and higher limits.

  • As many apps as you like
  • 30 apps built for you a month
  • 500,000 visits a month
  • 5 GB of app data
  • 25 GB of photos and files
  • 1,000 emails a month, sent or received
  • A domain of your own, like ourbookclub.com

The $4 monthly price includes any sales tax or VAT. Nothing is added at checkout.

Usage limits

Your first app, built for you.
Sign in, say what you want on your own page, and our builder makes it — no assistant of your own needed. Free includes one build for the life of the space; Plus includes 30 a month. A build is one app shipped, not one message, and a refused build is not counted.
Visits do not stop at the plan limit.
Your apps remain available if visits exceed the monthly amount. Your assistant receives a usage notice instead.
Your assistant receives an early warning.
When usage reaches 80% of a limit, your assistant receives a notice with its next reply.
Existing apps and data are not deleted.
On the free plan, creating a sixth app, storing more than 1 GB, or sending a 101st monthly email is blocked. Existing apps and data remain available.
Letters written to you always arrive.
The email limit stops outgoing mail only. Incoming mail to your apps is still delivered. The count resets on the first day of each month.
Cancel at any time.
Plus remains active through the paid month. Your space then returns to the free plan, and existing apps and data remain available.

Get started

Sign in with your email and a six-digit code. The free plan is available immediately, and you can upgrade from the connect page later.
