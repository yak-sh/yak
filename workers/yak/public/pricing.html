What it costs

Start free and stay free if five apps is all you want. When you want more, it's four dollars a month.

The two plans

Free

For a few apps of your own.

  • 5 apps
  • 50,000 visits a month
  • 1 GB of app data
  • 100 emails a month
  • Your own address at yourname.yaks.app

$4 a month

Called Plus. For when five isn't enough.

  • As many apps as you like
  • 500,000 visits a month
  • 5 GB of app data
  • 25 GB of photos and files
  • 1,000 emails a month
  • A domain of your own, like ourbookclub.com

Prices are in US dollars. Where your country charges sales tax or VAT, it's added at checkout, so what you pay may be a little more than the number above.

How we treat the limits

Visitors are never turned away.
If more people come than your plan counts, your apps keep working. We'll tell your assistant you're near the line rather than closing the door on your guests.
You get told before you get stopped.
At four fifths of any limit, your assistant hears about it on its next reply — so it can say something before you run into anything.
Nothing is deleted for going over.
On the free plan a sixth app waits, and so does data past a gigabyte and the hundred-and-first email. What you've already made stays exactly where it is.
Cancel whenever you like.
Plus runs to the end of the month you've paid for, and then your space is on the free plan again. Your apps and everything in them stay yours.

Where to start

Sign in with your email — we send a six-digit code, so there's no password to keep track of — and your free space is waiting. You can move to Plus from your own page later, whenever you want to.
