Acceptable use

Last updated 3 September 2026

This is a small, friendly place for making small, friendly things. The list below is short because most of it goes without saying.

Please don't

  • Break the law, or use an app here to hurt somebody.
  • Put anything here that sexualises a child. This one has no second chance: we remove it, close the account, and report it.
  • Harass, threaten, or campaign against people — including through an open app that anyone can write in.
  • Post other people's work, writing or pictures when you've no right to.
  • Host malware, phishing pages, or anything pretending to be a bank, a company, or a person it isn't.
  • Use the invitations to send bulk mail to people who never asked for it.
  • Try to get into somebody else's space, or go poking at the walls between them.
  • Mine cryptocurrency, run a proxy or a botnet, or treat this as free storage for files nothing here serves.
  • Hammer the place hard enough that other people can't use it.

What we do about it

Usually we write to you first and ask. Something clearly harmful we take down straight away, and the account with it. Where we can tell you why, we will.

If you see something

Tell us at hello@yaks.app. Say where it is and what's wrong, and we'll look the same day where we can.
