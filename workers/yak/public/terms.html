Sharing

Every app has a setting for who may see it. Private means you and the people you've invited. Public means anyone with the link can read it. Open means anyone with the link can write in it too. Which one to pick is your call, and you can change it whenever you like — but a link you've already sent out is out in the world, so choose the open ones thoughtfully.
