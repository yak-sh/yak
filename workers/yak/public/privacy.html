Privacy policy

Last updated 3 September 2026

We keep as little as we can, and we're happy to tell you all of it. This page is the whole list.

Who holds it

Yak Shaving LLC
971 S Centerville Rd, #2009
Sturgis, Michigan 49091
United States

Questions, corrections, or a request to delete something all go to hello@yaks.app.

What we keep

  • Your email address. It's how you sign in, how we send your code, and how a friend invites you to their app. We don't show it to other people — an app shows a name, never an address.
  • The name you chose. When you first sign in we ask what you'd like to be called. Skip the question and we use the front of your address. That name is what appears beside things you write in an app other people can see.
  • A session cookie. One, so you stay signed in. The cookies page has the details.
  • Your sign-in codes, briefly. A code lasts ten minutes and five guesses. We never store the digits themselves, only a one-way scramble of them, so nobody reading our records can sign in as you.
  • What's in your apps. Everything you and the people you invited write — text, lists, photos and any other file uploaded — along with who wrote it and when.
  • The apps themselves. The files your assistant writes to build them.
  • Reports when something breaks. If a page or a request fails, we record what happened: the message, where in the code it came from, the address being visited, and which version of the app was running. That report goes to your assistant so it can fix the thing. It isn't a record of what you were up to.

What we don't keep

No advertising profile, no history of where else you've been, no location, no contact list, and no payment details — there's nothing to pay for yet. We don't sell anything to anyone, ever.

Where it lives

All of it sits on Cloudflare, who host this whole platform: their workers run the code, their storage holds each app's data and files, and their network carries every request. They handle it on our behalf and keep their own operational records of requests, the way any host does. That network is worldwide, so the nearest copy to you may sit in a different country than the nearest copy to somebody else.

Email

Sign-in codes and invitations go out through Cloudflare Email Sending. Those are the only letters we send you unasked, and each one is something you or a friend set in motion.

Counting visits

We run no analytics of our own and no advertising trackers. Cloudflare gives us the ordinary request counts any host sees.

One honest note: Cloudflare currently attaches its own Web Analytics script to pages served from here. It counts page views, sets no cookie, and doesn't follow you elsewhere. We didn't add it, and we're switching it off.

Who can see your things

That's yours to set, one app at a time. Private means you and the members you invited. Public means anyone who has the link can read it. Open means anyone who has the link can write in it as well. Whoever you invite can see what's in that app.

Your assistant sees the apps you connected it to, and acts as you when it does. On our side, we look at what's in your apps only when you ask us for help with something, or when we have to in order to keep the place running or to answer the law.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account here. Delete an app and its data and files go with it. Close a space — ask your assistant to delete it and confirm the link we email you, or do it yourself while signed in — and its apps, everything they saved, their files and any domain aimed at them go too, and the address goes back into circulation. Ask us to close your account and we remove the rest, the record of you included; ordinary backups and server records age out on their own in the weeks after.

What you can ask for

Write to hello@yaks.app and ask for a copy of what we hold, a correction, or deletion, and we'll do it. Depending on where you live the law may give you those rights formally — in the UK and the EU, in California, and elsewhere. We'd rather just do it than argue about which rule applies.

Children

This isn't built for children, and it isn't for anyone under 13. If a child's information has ended up here, tell us and we'll remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the page and the date at the top change with it, and we'll email you when the change is one that matters.

Made with care by a small herd. It's early days here — thanks for coming by.
